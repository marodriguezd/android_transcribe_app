Two Activity-leak hardenings: SettingsManager.getSystemPrompt and applyDictionary now route through getContext() (which calls .getApplicationContext()), so a long-lived SettingsManager cannot leak any Activity reference handed in via constructor. Defensive code-review follow-up on top of v0.8.6 release hardening.

E2E verification on Samsung A059 (Android 16 / SDK 36): the rebuilt v0.8.6 source APK successfully transcoded both reference fixtures via the 0.6B Parakeet engine. dots.wav produced the Steve Jobs "connecting the dots" speech (588 chars vs 586-char spec, matches content); jfk.wav produced the clip-byte-for-byte match with the transcribe-rs/tests/parakeet.rs fixture ("And so, my fellow Americans, ask not what your country can do for you. Ask what you can do for your country." — 108 chars).

Known issue (deferred to v0.8.8): the Java manual RIFF/WAVE reader in TranscribeFileActivity threw `IOException("RIFF size too small: -1304424192")` on dots.wav and "Unexpected EOF in chunk header" on jfk.wav under the 0.6B path. End-to-end transcription still succeeded because the MediaExtractor fallback on the app-owned path /data/data/dev.notune.transcribe/files/ resolves the URI without going through the storage access framework. Path resolution: app reads WAV from `files/Download/` (app sandbox) using `cat /sdcard/Download/X.wav | run-as ... tee /data/user/0/dev.notune.transcribe/files/X.wav`. The EOF quirk in the manual reader remains observably logged but does not affect output correctness.
